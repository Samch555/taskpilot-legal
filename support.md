# TaskPilot Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email **[samch2347@gmail.com](mailto:samch2347@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within
two business days. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings → About in
TaskPilot, or the App Store page).

## Common questions

**Recording says it can't hear me.**
Make sure TaskPilot is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition: iOS Settings → TaskPilot. You can also
type your notes in the "Your note" box on the Record tab.

**I don't get task reminders.**
Turn on Task Alerts in TaskPilot → Settings → Notifications, and check that notifications are allowed in
iOS Settings → Notifications → TaskPilot. Focus modes can also silence them.

**How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?**
TaskPilot → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel TaskPilot Pro?**
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TaskPilot Pro → Cancel Subscription. Apple handles all billing; we can't
cancel or refund on your behalf, but you can request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Calendar or Reminders sync isn't working.**
Check TaskPilot → Settings → Calendar & Reminders, and that TaskPilot has Full Access in iOS Settings → Privacy &
Security → Calendars / Reminders. Tap "Sync Now" to sync immediately.

**How do I delete my data?**
Your tasks are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for
details.
